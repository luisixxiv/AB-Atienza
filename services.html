Questions

What people ask us

Click a question to open it.

Do you design and build?+

Yes. We draw the plan, show it in 2D and 3D, then build it and handle the finishing works.

Can I see my house before it is built?+

Yes. We prepare 2D layouts, 3D modeling and rendering, and digital walkthroughs.

Do you help with financing?+

Ask us about Pag-IBIG and bank financing. We promote a Build Now, Pay Later option. Contact us to check what applies to your project.

Can you renovate my existing house?+

Yes. We handle renovation works, and we can draw as-built plans of your existing building first.

Where do you work?+

We are based in Lucena City, Quezon. Contact us to confirm if we can take your location.

Do you sell construction materials?+

Our sister business, AB Atienza Hardware and Construction Supplies, sells materials such as concrete hollow blocks and aggregates.
